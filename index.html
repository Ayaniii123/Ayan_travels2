❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Help
Can I create my own account?

Yes. Use Create Account on the login screen, then sign in with your email and password.

Can I book a hotel from Ayan Travels?

You can submit the available booking form. Hotel availability and final confirmation should be verified with the hotel before travel.

Can I get directions to a destination?

Yes. Select a destination and use the route feature. Google Maps can also be opened for navigation.

Are hotel prices guaranteed?

No. Prices shown on the website are displayed as listed in the app and may change. Confirm the final price before payment.
